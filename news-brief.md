## 1. LEAD — COP31 collapses: no climate deal (Antalya, Turkiye)

- **Headline:** COP31 COLLAPSES
- **What happened:** The UN climate summit in Antalya ended on Sunday 22 Nov
  with no overarching agreement. After two weeks of talks and two days of
  overtime — the longest COP in history — the EU, UK, Panama, the Marshall
  Islands, Norway and others rejected the draft "Antalya Pact" because it did
  not mention fossil fuels at all. COP31 president Murat Kurum conceded there
  was no consensus. It is the first major breakdown of climate talks since
  COP15 in Copenhagen in 2009. The US was absent, having pulled out of
  international climate action under President Trump.
- **Why it matters:** The world is projected to overshoot the 1.5 C Paris
  target and the summit produced no plan to close the gap, no transition
  language and only $142m in new funding. More than 100 countries had pushed
  to keep fossil-fuel transition language in.
- **Sources:** CNN, Le Monde, NPR/WCMU, phys.org (AFP), IPS/Global Issues —
  all 22–23 Nov 2026.

## 2. Ukraine war: Kyiv flats hit, Russian ships burning

- **Headline:** KYIV FLATS HIT
- **What happened:** A Russian drone struck a five-storey apartment block in
  the Kyiv region early Sunday, partly collapsing it; at least 14 people were

- **Why it matters:** The war is spilling beyond Ukraine — into NATO-adjacent

- **Sources:** Reuters, BBC, CNN, Sky News, Finwire — 22–23 Nov 2026.

## 3. Gulf war widens: Iran hits Bahrain, tankers struck in Hormuz

- **Headline:** GULF WAR WIDENS
- **What happened:** Iran escalated attacks over the weekend: its Revolutionary
  Guard said it hit US Patriot radars at the Al-Riffa and Awali bases in
  Bahrain; a strike on a civilian industrial facility in Bahrain wounded two
  people and started a fire. Saudi Arabia intercepted a Houthi drone aimed at
  the Riyadh area and a ballistic missile in the west. Two tankers were
  reported struck in the Strait of Hormuz, through which a fifth of the
  world's oil passes; about 25 tankers have been hit in the past week and oil
  flows through the strait have dropped sharply. Iran also said it shot down
  another US MQ-1 drone. Iran's foreign minister said Tehran remains open to
  a deal with the US.
- **Why it matters:** The war that began 28 Feb 2026 is now hitting Gulf
  states, US bases and global oil shipping at once; the Strait of Hormuz is a
  global energy chokepoint.
- **Sources:** Al Jazeera, AFP via DailyFoxNews, political.org — 22–23 Nov 2026.

## 4. Indonesia: candy factory fire, about 50 missing

- **Headline:** FACTORY FIRE HORROR
- **What happened:** A fire broke out at 5pm local time Saturday at the PT
  Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory (Milkita brand) in Cikande, Serang,
  Banten, western Java. About 300 workers were inside. Police recovered seven
  body bags and rescuers were still searching for about 50 missing workers on
  Sunday; the victims were burned beyond recognition and identification by DNA
  could take two to three weeks. At least three confirmed dead, four injured.
- **Why it matters:** One of the deadliest industrial disasters in Indonesia
  in years; raises questions about factory safety for a workforce of thousands.
- **Sources:** Reuters, AP via Fox News, Northland News Radio — 22–23 Nov 2026.

- **Why it matters:** The first cabinet-level resignation over AI cheating in
